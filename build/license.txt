CoreShift Terms, Privacy and Audit Notice

By installing and using CoreShift, you agree that:

1. CoreShift is a local desktop tool. Features that use MySQL, community chat, or audit logging require a database configured by the app owner.
2. After you accept the in-app notice and sign in, CoreShift records your account username, a random installation identifier, local network IP address, device hostname, and login time in the configured MySQL audit log.
3. For fraud prevention and access auditing, CoreShift records a one-way SHA-256 hash derived from the system UUID. The raw hardware UUID is not stored. CoreShift does not intentionally collect a public internet IP address.
4. VirusTotal file and URL checks are only performed after you explicitly request them. Files may be submitted to VirusTotal according to its service terms.
5. Do not use CoreShift to violate game, service, or local laws and policies.

If you do not agree, do not install or use CoreShift.
